# Olist E-Commerce Analysis

## Overview
Exploratory data analysis of 100,000+ orders from Olist, Brazil's largest 
e-commerce platform covering the period 2016-2018.

## Business Questions Answered
1. How has order volume and revenue grown over time?
2. Which states generate the most revenue and orders?
3. What is the average delivery time and how many orders are delayed?

## Key Findings
- Olist grew 20x in order volume within just 12 months
- Sao Paulo alone drives ~37% of total revenue
- Top 3 states (SP, RJ, MG) account for ~62% of total revenue
- Average delivery time is 12 days
- 6.8% of orders are delayed past estimated delivery date

## Tools Used
Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn

## Dataset
Kaggle — Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist  
100k+ orders across 9 tables
